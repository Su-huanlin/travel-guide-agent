# Example: 2026 North Xinjiang comfort photography drive

Reference project pattern (not a dependency): `2026北疆舒适摄影自驾指南`.

## Locked brief (summary)

- Dates: 2026-07-21 → 2026-08-01  
- Party: 2 adults, 1 room  
- Style: photography + comfort; budget secondary  
- Transport: Beijing→Altay flight; Altay rental round-trip; Altay→Yining late flight; Yining→Urumqi airport one-way rental; Urumqi→Beijing direct  
- Exclude (user-locked): Hemu, Kalajun, Zhaosu wetland/Tianma, Nalati park, Tekes overnight, Devil City, Karamay  
- Duku: north segment only via **Qiaerma** entry  
- Tangbula: free S315 mainline only  

## Design moves that mattered

1. **Skip long empty arcs** with Altay→Yining flight.  
2. **Kanas 2 nights inside park** near transfer hub; exit shuttle not seat-bookable → conservative morning.  
3. **Default 二进 ticket** when stay ≫ official 一进 window unless park confirms in writing.  
4. **Split Tangbula and Duku** when calendar allows; if calendar slips, merge transfer+S315 on approach day, keep Duku morning alone.  
5. **Realtime replan** when still in Zhaosu on 7/30: freeze missed transfer day; force tonight Qiaerma; tomorrow Duku.
